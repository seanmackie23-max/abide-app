**Follows:** a paper in Christian spirituality (discernment and vocation).

**Frame:** a school of discernment in seven lessons: listen (Samuel), know your own heart (Teresa's castle), notice consolation and desolation (Ignatius's rules), review each day (the examen), decide (Ignatius's election), go on in darkness (John of the Cross), and decide with others (Acts 15).

**Real questions:** How do I know God's will for my life? Does God have a plan for me? How do I make big decisions? What is discernment? What is the examen? What are consolation and desolation? What is the dark night of the soul? What if God seems silent? How do I know my vocation? Should I ask for a sign?

**Builds on:** *Silence*, *How to pray*, *Mystics*, *Reformation* (Ignatius's Principle and Foundation, read). **Read elsewhere:** Principle and Foundation (*The Reformation*).

## Sessions

1. **Speak, for your servant hears.** *Finding:* The boy Samuel needed an old priest to help him recognise God's call: discernment begins with listening, and with help. *Read:* 1 Samuel 3:1–10. *Compare:* Isaiah 6:8. *Lecture:* calling in the Bible; vocation (point back to *Work and money*). *Learn:* new `1-samuel-3-10`. *Look:* the calling of Samuel. *Music:* none.
2. **The interior castle.** *Finding:* Teresa of Ávila pictured the soul as a castle of many rooms with God at the centre: knowing yourself is the first door. *Read:* *Interior Castle*, First Mansions 1 (Stanbrook). *Compare:* Psalm 139:23–24 (point back). *Lecture:* Teresa's life (Ávila, Carmelite reform, 1582); self-knowledge and humility. *Learn:* `teresa-let-nothing-disturb-thee`. *Look:* St Teresa (NGA: flag). *Music:* none.
3. **Consolation and desolation.** *Finding:* Ignatius, recovering from a cannonball wound, noticed that some daydreams left him peaceful and others empty: the beginning of his rules for discerning the spirits. *Read:* *Spiritual Exercises*, Rules for Discernment (First Week) 1–14. *Compare:* Galatians 5:22–23 (point back). *Lecture:* Ignatius's life; consolation and desolation explained plainly; 'never change a decision in desolation'. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* St Ignatius. *Music:* none.
4. **The examen.** *Finding:* Ignatius asked his followers never to skip one prayer: a daily review of the day to notice God's presence and one's own responses. *Read:* the Examen (Exercises 43). *Compare:* Lamentations 3:22–23. *Lecture:* the five steps; Marcus Aurelius's evening review (point back). *Learn:* none new. *Look:* a figure at evening prayer. *Music:* none.
5. **Making a choice.** *Finding:* For big decisions Ignatius offers ways: imagine advising a stranger, imagine your deathbed, list reasons, and seek peace. *Read:* the Second Week election (169–188). *Compare:* Proverbs 3:5–6 and 15:22. *Lecture:* decision-making; signs and fleeces (Judges 6) and why the tradition is wary. *Learn:* `proverbs-3-5` (if new). *Look:* none. *Music:* none.
6. **The case against: does God have a plan for me?** *Finding:* Critics say talk of 'God's will for my life' is wishful projection, and that it paralyses people looking for a perfect blueprint; even many Christians say God gives freedom and wisdom, not a hidden map. *Read:* John of the Cross, *Dark Night* I.1 and II.5. *Compare:* Ecclesiastes 9:11 (point back). *Lecture:* the objection at full strength: the sceptic (projection), and the Christian critic (Garry Friesen's *Decision Making and the Will of God*, Kevin DeYoung's *Just Do Something*, our own words); the dark night when God seems silent. *Debate:* blueprint; wisdom; the sceptic. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* a night scene. *Music:* none.
7. **It seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us: the verdict.** *Finding:* God's will is mostly already known (love God and neighbour); the rest is found by prayer, wisdom, counsel and peace, and confirmed by living it. *Read:* Philippians 1:9–11, with Romans 12:1–2 (*The first Christians*) and Acts 15:28 (*One root, two branches*) pointed back. *Compare:* Newman's 'Lead, Kindly Light' (1833, PD). *Lecture:* discerning with others: spiritual direction, friends, church. *Learn:* new `romans-12-2` (the verse alone). *Look:* a road at dawn. *Music:* none.

**Objection:** session 6.
