**Follows:** a paper in theology (the theological virtues).

**Frame:** the three virtues Paul named that look beyond us: faith (trusting God), hope (expecting his promise), love (friendship with God and neighbour). One session each with a case and a practice, then their enemies (doubt, despair, indifference), and 'the greatest of these'.

**Real questions:** What are the theological virtues? What is faith? Is faith believing without evidence? What is hope, and how is it different from optimism? What is Christian love? What is agape? Why is love the greatest? Can you choose to have faith? What is despair? How do I grow in love?

**Builds on:** *The four virtues*, *Pascal* (the heart), *Abraham* (Hebrews 11:8–16). **Read elsewhere:** 1 Corinthians 13 (memory and *How we got here*), Hebrews 11:8–16 (*Abraham*).

## Sessions

1. **Three that remain.** *Finding:* Paul names faith, hope and love as what remains when everything else passes; Augustine built a whole handbook of the faith on them. *Read:* Augustine, *Enchiridion* 1–8. *Compare:* 1 Corinthians 13:13 (point back). *Lecture:* theological and cardinal virtues; gifts, not achievements. *Learn:* `corinthians-love-is-patient`. *Look:* Faith, Hope and Charity personified. *Music:* none.
2. **Faith.** *Finding:* Faith in the Bible is trust in a person shown by action, not belief without evidence: 'the assurance of things hoped for'. *Read:* Hebrews 11:1–7 and 11:32–12:2. *Compare:* Aquinas II–II q.4 a.1. *Lecture:* faith and reason (point back); faith and doubt; Newman's 'ten thousand difficulties do not make one doubt' (PD). *Learn:* new `hebrews-11-1`. *Look:* Abraham or Noah. *Music:* none.
3. **Hope.** *Finding:* Hope is not optimism but confident expectation of what God has promised; it 'does not disappoint'. *Read:* Romans 5:1–5 and 8:24–25. *Compare:* Aquinas II–II q.17 a.2 and Péguy's little girl Hope (our own words). *Lecture:* hope and despair; Václav Havel's 'hope is not the conviction that something will turn out well' (short quotation); Dante's gate (point back). *Learn:* none new. *Look:* a dawn landscape. *Music:* `o-radiant-dawn`.
4. **Love.** *Finding:* Christian love (*agape*) is willing the good of the other, even the enemy, because God first loved us; Aquinas called it friendship with God. *Read:* 1 John 4:7–21. *Compare:* Aquinas II–II q.23 a.1. *Lecture:* eros, philia, agape (point ahead to *Love, friendship and marriage*); Anders Nygren (our own words). *Learn:* `1-john-4-16` (if new elsewhere). *Look:* Charity. *Music:* `ubi-caritas`.
5. **The case against: faith as belief without evidence.** *Finding:* Critics define faith as believing what you cannot prove and call it a vice; W. K. Clifford said it is wrong always to believe on insufficient evidence. *Read:* Clifford, 'The Ethics of Belief' (1877) (point back if read in *Pascal*: then read Dawkins's charge in our own words). *Compare:* Hebrews 11:1 recalled. *Lecture:* the objection at full strength (Clifford; Dawkins; Boghossian, our own words); replies (James; trust in persons; Plantinga's basic beliefs, our own words). *Debate:* Clifford; James; Aquinas. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* none. *Music:* none.
6. **The enemies of hope and love.** *Finding:* The tradition named despair and *acedia* (spiritual sloth, not caring) as the enemies of hope and love, and they are the sicknesses of our age. *Read:* Aquinas II–II q.20 (despair) and q.35 (acedia), selected. *Compare:* Psalm 42 (point back). *Lecture:* acedia (Evagrius, point ahead to *The seven deadly sins*); depression is not sin (pastoral care). *Learn:* none new. *Look:* a figure in melancholy. *Music:* none.
7. **The greatest of these: the verdict.** *Finding:* Faith and hope end when we see; love never ends; so the whole of the good life is learning to love. *Read:* 1 Corinthians 13:8–13 (point back). *Compare:* Augustine, *Homilies on 1 John* 7.8 ('love, and do what you will') (NPNF). *Lecture:* practising the three (a table of practices). *Learn:* none new. *Look:* none. *Music:* `jesu-joy`.

**Objection:** session 5.

**Traditions:** faith and works (point back to *The Reformation*), assurance (Reformed), hope (Orthodox) side by side.

**Voices:** Josef Pieper; C. S. Lewis; Charles Péguy; Benedict XVI, *Deus Caritas Est* and *Spe Salvi* (our own words); Václav Havel.
