**Follows:** a first-year paper in religious studies and theology of religions.

**Frame:** three answers to one question, laid out as a table and tested in turn: exclusivism (only through Christ, known explicitly), inclusivism (only through Christ, but perhaps unknown), pluralism (many paths up one mountain). The journey meets Judaism, Islam, Hinduism and Buddhism in their own words before judging.

**Real questions:** Do all religions lead to God? Is Christianity the only way? What happens to people who never heard of Jesus? Do Muslims and Christians worship the same God? What do Jews think of Jesus? What do Muslims believe about Jesus? Is Buddhism compatible with Christianity? Can I be a Christian and practise yoga or meditation? Isn't it arrogant to say one religion is true? What does the Catholic Church teach?

**Builds on:** *Is there a God?* (Acts 17), *When Athens met Jerusalem* (Justin), *One root, two branches*, *The world church*. **Read elsewhere:** Acts 17, Justin, Qur'an Sura 4:157 (*The last week*), Sura 14 (*Abraham*), Sura 19 (if used in *Who was Jesus?*: settle).

## Sessions

1. **No other name?** *Finding:* The New Testament says salvation is through Jesus alone, and also that God has never left himself without witness: both claims must be held. *Read:* Acts 4:12 and John 14:6, with Malachi 1:11 and Acts 14:15–17. *Compare:* Acts 17 (point back). *Lecture:* the three positions as a table; key texts for each. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* a crossroads or signpost. *Music:* none.
2. **Judaism.** *Finding:* Jews and Christians share Scripture and the God of Abraham; Maimonides saw Christianity and Islam as preparing the world for the Messiah. *Read:* Maimonides (our own words / PD). *Compare:* Romans 11 (point back). *Lecture:* Jewish views of Jesus; point back to *One root, two branches*. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* a synagogue or rabbi. *Music:* none.
3. **Islam.** *Finding:* The Qur'an honours Jesus as Messiah, born of a virgin, a prophet, but denies his divinity and crucifixion: close and far at once. *Read:* Qur'an 3:45–59 and 19:16–36 (Rodwell). *Compare:* John 1:14 (point back). *Lecture:* Islam's origins; the same God? (Miroslav Volf's *Allah* and its critics, our own words); Llull and Cusa's dialogues. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* Mary and the angel. *Music:* none.
4. **Hinduism and Buddhism.** *Finding:* The Gita teaches devotion and action without attachment; the Buddha taught the end of suffering through the end of craving; Christians have found both deep and different. *Read:* *Bhagavad Gita* II.47, XII.13–20 (Arnold) and *Dhammapada* I.1–5 (Müller). *Compare:* Matthew 6:19–21 (point back). *Lecture:* Hindu and Buddhist core beliefs fairly; Christian dialogue (Bede Griffiths, Thomas Merton, our own words); yoga and meditation questions. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* an Asian landscape (NGA: flag). *Music:* none.
5. **The case against: the arrogance of truth.** *Finding:* Pluralists say that claiming one religion is true is arrogant and culturally determined (born in Riyadh you would be Muslim); John Hick argued all religions are responses to one Real. *Read:* the parable of the blind men and the elephant (Udana 6.4, PD translation) as the pluralist image. *Compare:* Hick (our own words). *Lecture:* the objection at full strength: Hick; the accident of birth; replies (Lesslie Newbigin: the elephant story claims to see what the blind men cannot; Alvin Plantinga on the accident of birth applying to pluralism too; our own words). *Debate:* Hick; Newbigin; a Buddhist. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* an elephant (NGA: flag). *Music:* none.
6. **Those who never heard.** *Finding:* Christians have long asked about the unevangelised: the traditions answer with Christ's descent to the dead, God's mercy, 'invincible ignorance' and hope. *Read:* 1 Peter 3:18–20 and Romans 2:12–16. *Compare:* *Nostra Aetate* 2 (our own words) and C. S. Lewis's Emeth in *The Last Battle* (our own words). *Lecture:* Catholic (*Lumen Gentium* 16), Orthodox, evangelical views side by side. *Learn:* none new. *Look:* the Harrowing of Hell (point back if used: another). *Music:* none.
7. **Witness and respect: the verdict.** *Finding:* Christians can hold that Christ is the truth and still learn from, respect and love people of other faiths: confidence and humility together. *Read:* 1 Peter 3:15–16 (point back) with Acts 10:34–35 (point back). *Compare:* Francis and the Sultan (point back to *Crusades*). *Lecture:* how to talk with people of other faiths; religious freedom (point back). *Learn:* none new. *Look:* St Francis. *Music:* none.

**Objection:** session 5.

**Traditions:** Catholic (*Nostra Aetate*, *Dominus Iesus*), Orthodox, evangelical, liberal Protestant side by side; Jewish and Muslim views of Christianity.
