**Follows:** a first-year paper on the earliest Church and Paul.

**Frame:** a small sect that became a quarter of the empire in three centuries; the journey follows its growth outward as Acts does, Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, the ends of the earth, and asks at each step why it spread.

**Real questions:** Who were the first Christians? Who was Paul? Did Paul invent Christianity? Why did Christianity spread so fast? What did early Christians actually do? Who were the women in the early Church? What did Romans think of them? What is the Didache? How many Christians were there?

**Builds on:** *The last week* (the resurrection witnesses). **Read elsewhere:** Acts 2 (*The whole story*, *Time made holy*); Acts 12 (*How to pray*); Acts 17 (*Is there a God?*); 1 Corinthians 13 (*How we got here*, others); Galatians 3 and Romans 4 (*Abraham*); Philemon (*How we got here*).

## Sessions

1. **Witnesses.** *Answers:* Who were the first Christians? What happened after Easter? *Finding:* The Church began as a group of Jewish witnesses in Jerusalem who said they had seen the risen Jesus and would not stop saying so.
   *Read:* Acts 1:1–14. *Compare:* Acts 4:32–37 (all things in common).
   *Lecture:* Luke as historian (Acts 1:1 with Luke 1:1–4, pointing back); the Twelve, James, Mary; Pentecost recalled. *Debate:* how reliable is Acts?
   *Learn:* new `acts-1-8`. *Look:* the Ascension (the Koerbecke is used), or the apostles. *Music:* `veni-creator`.
2. **The first martyr and the persecutor.** *Answers:* Who was Stephen? Who was Paul and what happened to him? *Finding:* The first martyr's death scattered the Church, and its fiercest persecutor became its greatest missionary.
   *Read:* Acts 7:54–8:3 and Acts 9:1–22. *Compare:* Galatians 1:11–17 (Paul's own account; 1:18–24 is read in *What is the Bible?*: point back).
   *Lecture:* Saul of Tarsus: Pharisee, Roman citizen, tentmaker; three accounts of the conversion compared. *Debate:* conversion or calling?
   *Learn:* new `galatians-2-20`. *Look:* the Conversion of St Paul, or the stoning of Stephen. *Music:* `mendelssohn-elijah` (or *St Paul* if added).
3. **To the ends of the earth.** *Answers:* Why did Christianity spread so fast? *Finding:* Roman roads, Greek language and Jewish synagogues carried the message; Paul's letters built a network of small households across the empire.
   *Read:* Acts 28:16–31 (Paul in Rome) and Romans 16:1–16 (the greetings). *Compare:* Lucian, *The Death of Peregrinus* 11–13 (a pagan's mocking account of how Christians cared for their own).
   *Lecture:* Paul's journeys (map); house churches; Phoebe, Prisca and Junia; numbers (Stark's growth rate, our own words). *Debate:* why did it grow? (Stark, Hurtado, Gibbon's five causes.)
   *Learn:* `corinthians-love-is-patient`. *Look:* St Paul (the Tintoretto is used). *Music:* `for-all-the-saints`.
4. **How they lived.** *Answers:* What did early Christians actually do? What is the Didache? *Finding:* They were known for what they did: caring for the sick, refusing infanticide and the arena, sharing a meal and praying three times a day.
   *Read:* Didache 1–4 and 9–10. *Compare:* Romans 12:1–21 (or point back).
   *Lecture:* the two ways; baptism and the meal (point ahead to *What happens at church* as the hall only); plague and care (Dionysius of Alexandria in Eusebius VII.22). *Debate:* why did their ethics draw people?
   *Learn:* new `romans-12-21` (if not used earlier). *Look:* charity, or St Lawrence giving to the poor. *Music:* `ubi-caritas`.
5. **What Rome saw.** *Answers:* What did Romans think of them? Why were they suspected? *Finding:* To Roman officials Christians were a stubborn superstition; to their critics, a threat to the gods and the family; even enemies noticed their discipline.
   *Read:* Pliny, *Letters* X.96–97 (moved from *Who was Jesus?*). *Compare:* Minucius Felix, *Octavius* 8–9 (the pagan charges) (ANF IV).
   *Lecture:* the charges (atheism, cannibalism, incest); Galen's grudging respect (our own words); Celsus (point ahead to Hall IV as the hall). *Debate:* why did Rome distrust them?
   *Learn:* `matthew-the-beatitudes` recalled; new `1-peter-3-15`. *Look:* a Roman official, or early Christian art. *Music:* `o-radiant-dawn`.
6. **The case against: did Paul invent Christianity?** *Answers:* Did Paul change Jesus's message? Was Christianity Paul's creation? *Finding:* Paul handed on what he received within a few years of the cross, and his letters assume Jesus's death, resurrection and teaching; the question is whether he transformed them.
   *Read:* Galatians 2:1–14 (Paul and Peter in Antioch). *Compare:* 1 Corinthians 11:23 and 15:3 (what Paul 'received'), and Nietzsche, *The Antichrist* §42 (Mencken, 1918) on Paul.
   *Lecture:* the objection at full strength: Nietzsche, William Wrede, Hyam Maccoby (our own words); replies from James Dunn and N. T. Wright (our own words); Paul and James. *Debate:* founder, faithful interpreter or both?
   *Learn:* `cor-15-3`. *Look:* Peter and Paul together. *Music:* `bach-magnificat`.
7. **The verdict.** *Answers:* So how did they change an empire? *Finding:* A message about a crucified and risen Lord, carried by small communities that lived it, spread without power, money or force: historians still argue why.
   *Read:* the *Letter to Diognetus* 5–6 is reserved for *The martyrs and the empire*; use 1 Thessalonians 1:1–10 (the earliest Christian writing). *Compare:* Tertullian, *Apology* 39 ('see how they love one another') (ANF III).
   *Lecture:* the growth figures; the faith beyond Rome (Edessa, Armenia, Ethiopia); what changed in the empire. *Debate:* the verdict.
   *Learn:* new `1-thessalonians-1-9`. *Look:* an early church or catacomb scene. *Music:* `for-all-the-saints`.

**Objection:** session 6, 'Paul invented Christianity', in Nietzsche's and Maccoby's terms.

**Traditions:** Peter and the papacy (Catholic and Orthodox views), the role of women (Phoebe, Junia), apostolic succession.

**Voices:** Rodney Stark; Larry Hurtado, *Destroyer of the Gods*; James Dunn; Hyam Maccoby; N. T. Wright, *Paul and the Faithfulness of God*; Robin Lane Fox, *Pagans and Christians*.

**Closing:** short answers; exam: 'Did Paul invent Christianity?'; 'Why did Christianity grow?'; 'How reliable is Acts?'.
