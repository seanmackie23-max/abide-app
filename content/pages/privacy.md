## In short

Rose Window keeps almost everything on your own device. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies. If you sign up for the daily email, we keep your email address to send it, and nothing else.

## What stays on your device

What you do in Rose Window (the days you keep, what you write, your journeys, what you learn by heart, your rule of life and your settings) is stored in your browser's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes it.

## The daily email

If you sign up, we store your email address and the date you confirmed it, so that we can send you one email each morning. We ask you to confirm by clicking a link first (double opt-in). The emails are sent through Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which processes your address on our behalf under a data processing agreement and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link; when you use it, we stop sending at once. You can also email us to have your address deleted. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

## Counting visits

At the moment Rose Window counts nothing: there is no analytics at all. If we switch counting on, we will use Plausible Analytics, hosted in the EU, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data or IP addresses, to count visits and a few actions (for example "a morning was kept" or "a journey session was finished"). Nothing you write would ever be counted or sent. We will update this page before we do. Legal basis then: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving Rose Window (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## The website and our small server

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, it briefly processes your IP address to deliver the pages, and may keep it in security logs. When you sign up for the email, your request passes through our small server on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which uses your IP address only to stop abuse and does not store it.

## Services you choose to use

- **Churches near you** (not switched on at present). When it is, and you search, your location, rounded to about 100 metres, is sent to OpenStreetMap's services to find churches. It is kept only on your device.
- **Music.** Music plays through Spotify's own player, which loads only when you choose to listen. Spotify's privacy policy then applies.
- **Links to other sites.** Some pages link to other websites (for example Project Gutenberg for a full text). Nothing is sent to them unless you follow the link.
- **Paintings** come from the National Gallery of Art, Washington, but are served by Rose Window itself.

## Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you (in practice, at most your email address), and have it corrected or deleted. You can withdraw your consent to the email at any time. You can complain to a data protection authority; ours is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HmbBfDI).
