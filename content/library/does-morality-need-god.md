## Why it matters for you

Every day you make choices about honesty, kindness and courage. Knowing that goodness is real, not just a preference, gives those choices weight, and gives you something solid to stand on when the world around you shifts. It also means every person you meet has a dignity no one can take away.

## The short answer

Almost everyone believes some things are truly wrong, not just disliked: cruelty to a child, betrayal of a friend. The question is what makes that true. Christians believe goodness is real because it is rooted in God. This is not a claim that atheists can't be good; plenty are. It is a question about what goodness rests on.

## What Christians believe

Every person is made "in God's image" (Genesis 1:27), which gives each human life a dignity no one can take away. And there is a moral law written on every heart (Romans 2:15), which is why people across every culture recognise courage, honesty, mercy and justice.

## The great teachers

**Aquinas** taught that there is a *natural law*: the basic principles of right action that human reason can discover, because they reflect the order God built into creation.

**C.S. Lewis**, in *The Abolition of Man* (1943), argued that objective values, which he called the *Tao*, are recognised across civilisations. He warned that an education which treats them as mere feelings produces "men without chests": clever people with no trained heart to stand between reason and appetite.

## The modern debate
tag: Strongest objections included

**Friedrich Nietzsche** proclaimed the "death of God" and saw more clearly than most that Christian morality might not survive without Christian belief. The historian **Tom Holland**, in *Dominion* (2019), argues that ideals like human rights and care for the weak are deeply Christian in origin.

Against this, an ancient challenge comes from Plato's *Euthyphro*: is something good because God commands it, or does God command it because it is good? Many secular philosophers argue that moral truths can stand on their own without God. The debate is live and worth following.
