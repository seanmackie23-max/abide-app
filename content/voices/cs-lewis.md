## In one idea

Christianity is either of no importance or of infinite importance; the one thing it cannot be is moderately important. Lewis was an atheist Oxford don who came to faith in his early thirties, partly through long conversations with his friend J.R.R. Tolkien.

## Why it matters now

No one has explained Christianity more clearly to people who don't believe it. His wartime radio talks became *Mere Christianity*, still one of the most-read Christian books in the world.

## Where to start

*Mere Christianity*; *The Abolition of Man* on objective values; *A Grief Observed* on loss; the Narnia books with your children.
