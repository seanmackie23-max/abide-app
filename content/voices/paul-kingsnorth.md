## In one idea

Modern technological culture, which he calls 'the Machine', is uprooting us from nature, place and the sacred. A former environmental activist and pagan, Kingsnorth found his way to Christianity and now writes about how to stay human in a digital age.

## Why it matters now

He speaks to people who sense that something is wrong with modern life, screens and endless growth, and are looking for roots.

## Where to start

*Against the Machine* (2025); his Substack *The Abbey of Misrule*; his essay on becoming a Christian, "The Cross and the Machine" (2021).

## Critics say

Some readers find his critique of technology too sweeping or pessimistic.
