## In one idea

Faith is not a comfortable opinion but a passionate, personal commitment, often summed up as a "leap". Kierkegaard attacked the cosy Christianity of his day, where everyone was Christian by birth and almost nobody by conviction.

## Why it matters now

Often called the father of existentialism, he wrote about anxiety, despair and the search for an authentic self long before psychology did. He speaks directly to people who feel they are drifting through life.

## Where to start

*Fear and Trembling* (on Abraham and Isaac) or *The Sickness unto Death* (on despair). A good introduction is *Provocations*, a collection of short extracts.

## Critics say

Some argue he set faith against reason too sharply.
