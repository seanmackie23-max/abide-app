## In one idea

Christianity is the great adventure, and orthodoxy is more thrilling than heresy. Journalist, novelist and creator of the Father Brown detective stories, Chesterton argued for faith with paradox and joy.

## Why it matters now

He shows that belief can be cheerful, funny and intellectually fearless. He reached Christianity by asking why the world felt like a story with an author.

## Where to start

*Orthodoxy* (1908), his own account of how he came to believe.
