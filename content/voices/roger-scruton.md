## In one idea

Beauty is as real as truth and goodness, and the experience of beauty is a call to the sacred. Scruton, who played the organ in his village church in Wiltshire, argued that we live in a culture that has fled from beauty, and that this leaves us spiritually homeless.

## Why it matters now

His BBC film *Why Beauty Matters* (2009) has helped many people put words to what they feel in an old church and miss in a modern shopping centre. He explains why sacred music, architecture and ritual are not decoration, but how people meet the transcendent.

## Where to start

The documentary *Why Beauty Matters*; then his books *Beauty* (2009) and *The Face of God* (2012).

## Critics say

Critics call his view of modern art nostalgic or elitist, and his political writing was often controversial. Pew presents his thinking on beauty and the sacred.
