## In one idea

Reason, not faith, should guide our lives, and the deepest spiritual experiences are available without religion. Harris was one of the 'Four Horsemen' of the New Atheism after *The End of Faith* (2004), and later founded the meditation app Waking Up.

## Why it matters now

He is the strongest honest opponent many seekers meet, and Abide takes him seriously. His debates with Jordan Peterson and William Lane Craig are where many people first heard the case for Christianity argued well.

## Where to start

*Waking Up* (2014); *The Moral Landscape* (2010); his 2018 debates with Jordan Peterson.

## Critics say

Christian critics argue that his morality of 'wellbeing' borrows Christian assumptions about human worth, and that meditation without God answers the question of how to be calm, but not of what life is for.
