## In one idea

Belief in God is making a surprising comeback among thinking people. For over fifteen years Brierley hosted *Unbelievable?*, a radio show and podcast where Christians and atheists debated face to face.

## Why it matters now

His book traces exactly the path Pew is built for: how figures like Peterson, Holland and Hirsi Ali, and many ordinary people, are reconsidering Christianity.

## Where to start

*The Surprising Rebirth of Belief in God* (2023); the podcast *The Re-Enchanting*.
