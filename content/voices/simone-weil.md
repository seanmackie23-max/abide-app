## In one idea

Attention is the purest form of prayer, and of love. A brilliant philosopher who worked in factories to share the lives of workers, Weil had profound experiences of Christ yet chose to stay on the threshold of the Church.

## Why it matters now

She is a voice for everyone who stands at the edge of faith, drawn but uncertain. Her writing on suffering, which she called "affliction", is among the deepest ever written.

## Where to start

*Waiting for God*, especially the essay on school studies and the love of God.
