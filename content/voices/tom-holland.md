## In one idea

Even the West's secular values come from Christianity. Holland grew up loving the gods of Greece and Rome, then realised that his own moral instincts, such as care for the weak and the dignity of every person, were not classical at all but Christian.

## Why it matters now

*Dominion* (2019) has led many sceptical readers to take Christianity seriously as the foundation of the world they live in. He also co-hosts the popular podcast *The Rest is History*.

## Where to start

*Dominion: The Making of the Western Mind*.

## Critics say

Some historians argue he underplays other sources of modern values, such as the Enlightenment or other religious traditions.
