## In one idea

There is no cheap grace. Faith costs something: it means following Christ in real life. Bonhoeffer helped lead the Confessing Church against the Nazis, was arrested, and was executed at Flossenbürg in April 1945, weeks before the war ended.

## Why it matters now

He shows faith that acts. His *Life Together*, written for an underground seminary, is a practical guide to Christian community, much like the Rule of Life at the heart of Abide.

## Where to start

*The Cost of Discipleship*, then *Life Together*, then *Letters and Papers from Prison*.
