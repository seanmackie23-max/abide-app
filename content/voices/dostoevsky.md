## In one idea

Freedom is a terrible gift, and only love can redeem it. Sentenced to death and reprieved at the last moment, then sent to a Siberian prison camp with only a New Testament, Dostoevsky wrote novels that put faith and doubt on trial.

## Why it matters now

In *The Brothers Karamazov* he gave the case against God to Ivan, and made it so powerful that readers still argue over whether the novel answers it. He shows what happens to people and societies when God is removed.

## Where to start

*Crime and Punishment*, then *The Brothers Karamazov*. Read the chapters "Rebellion" and "The Grand Inquisitor" if nothing else.
