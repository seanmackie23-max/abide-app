## In one idea

We learn what to desire by copying others, and rivalry turns communities against a scapegoat. Girard argued that the Bible, uniquely among ancient texts, takes the side of the innocent victim, and that the cross exposes the scapegoat mechanism for good.

## Why it matters now

His ideas explain online pile-ons, envy and polarisation with uncanny precision. Studying ancient myths led him, unexpectedly, back to Christian faith.

## Where to start

*I See Satan Fall Like Lightning* (1999), his most direct book on Christianity.

## Critics say

Some scholars think his theory explains too much with one idea.
