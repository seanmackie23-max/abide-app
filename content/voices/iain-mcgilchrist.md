## In one idea

Our culture has become dominated by a narrow, grasping way of seeing, linked to the brain's left hemisphere, and has lost the broader, relational vision that perceives meaning, beauty and the sacred. McGilchrist argues that the divine is not an optional extra but part of reality.

## Why it matters now

He gives seekers a scientifically informed language for why the modern world feels flat, and why religion, art and nature matter.

## Where to start

*The Master and His Emissary* (2009); *The Matter with Things* (2021).

## Critics say

Neuroscientists debate how far his hemisphere theory can be pushed. He describes himself as not belonging to any church.
