## In one idea

The psyche speaks in archetypes, and religious symbols are among its deepest expressions. The son of a Reformed pastor, Jung founded analytical psychology and took religion seriously as a living reality of the soul.

## Why it matters now

He influenced Peterson and many others, and his idea of "individuation", becoming whole, overlaps with Christian ideas of transformation. He is included because many seekers come to faith by way of his work.

## Where to start

*Memories, Dreams, Reflections*, his autobiography; *Modern Man in Search of a Soul*.

## Critics say

Jung treated God as a reality of the psyche, not as the God of Christian faith. Many Christians, and many psychologists, reject his views.
