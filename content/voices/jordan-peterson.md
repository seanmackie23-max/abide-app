## In one idea

The Bible's stories are maps of meaning: they encode hard-won wisdom about how to live, take responsibility and confront chaos. Peterson's lecture series on Genesis, released free online in 2017, drew millions of viewers, many of whom had never read the Bible.

## Why it matters now

He brought the Bible back into public conversation for a generation, especially young men. His focus on responsibility, truthful speech and meaning resonates with people who find church language foreign.

## Where to start

His *Biblical Series* lectures on Genesis (2017); his book *We Who Wrestle with God* (2024).

## Critics say

Many theologians argue he reads the Bible as psychology rather than as God's revelation, and he has been publicly ambivalent about whether he believes in God in the traditional sense. He is also a polarising public figure; Abide presents his reading of Scripture, not his wider views.
