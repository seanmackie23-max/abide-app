## In one idea

Humans are not purely rational: we are moral and religious creatures, made for community, the sacred and awe. Haidt, who describes himself as an atheist, argues that religion binds people together in ways secular society has struggled to replace.

## Why it matters now

His work on why the smartphone generation is so anxious makes a strong case for practices Abide offers: real community, awe, Sabbath-like breaks and spiritual elevation.

## Where to start

*The Righteous Mind* (2012); *The Anxious Generation* (2024), especially the chapter on spiritual elevation and degradation.
