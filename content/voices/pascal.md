## In one idea

"The heart has its reasons, which reason does not know." A scientific genius who invented an early calculator and helped found probability theory, Pascal had a dramatic encounter with God in 1654, which he sewed into his coat and kept for the rest of his life.

## Why it matters now

He saw how modern people distract themselves to avoid the big questions. His observation that our unhappiness comes from being unable to sit quietly in a room alone reads like a description of the smartphone.

## Where to start

*Pensées*, his unfinished notes for a defence of Christianity. Dip in anywhere.

## Critics say

His famous wager, that it is rational to bet on God, is often criticised as treating faith like a gamble. Pascal meant it as a first step, not a full argument.
