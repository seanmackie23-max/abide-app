## In one idea

Science and God are not rivals: science explains how the universe works, but it cannot explain why there is a universe or why it is intelligible at all. Lennox, an Oxford mathematician, has debated Richard Dawkins and Christopher Hitchens with unfailing courtesy.

## Why it matters now

He shows that a first-rate scientific mind can hold Christian faith with confidence, and he answers the questions sceptics actually ask.

## Where to start

*God's Undertaker: Has Science Buried God?* (2007); his 2007 debate with Richard Dawkins; *2084* (2020) on artificial intelligence.
