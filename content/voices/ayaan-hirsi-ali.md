## In one idea

A famous atheist who changed her mind. Raised Muslim, then an outspoken atheist and author of *Infidel* (2006), she announced in a 2023 essay, "Why I am now a Christian", that she had come to faith, both because she believed Western civilisation needs its Christian roots and because she found atheism could not meet her own spiritual needs.

## Why it matters now

Her story maps the journey of many seekers: starting with an argument about civilisation, ending with something personal.

## Where to start

Her essay "Why I am now a Christian" (UnHerd, November 2023); *Infidel* (2006).

## Critics say

Some critics ask whether defending Christianity as a civilisation is the same as believing it is true. She has said her faith is both.
