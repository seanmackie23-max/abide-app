## In one idea

The world is made of meaning: ancient symbols reveal patterns that join heaven and earth. A carver of Orthodox icons, Pageau explains the symbolic language of the Bible, the Church Fathers and old stories, from Genesis to fairy tales.

## Why it matters now

He helps modern people, trained to read everything literally, see the Bible as the early Christians did. His YouTube channel and podcast *The Symbolic World* reach a large audience of seekers.

## Where to start

*The Language of Creation* (2018) on Genesis; *The Symbolic World* podcast and videos.

## Critics say

Some readers find his symbolic approach hard to follow at first, or worry it can drift from the plain sense of the text.
