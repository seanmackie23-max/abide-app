## In one idea

Faith and reason are two roads to the same truth, because both come from God. Aquinas brought the philosophy of Aristotle into conversation with Christian faith and built the most complete system of theology ever written, the *Summa Theologiae*.

## Why it matters now

In an age that assumes religion and reason are enemies, Aquinas shows a mind that took every objection seriously. Each article of the *Summa* begins by stating the strongest arguments against his own view.

## Where to start

Start with Edward Feser's *Aquinas: A Beginner's Guide*, or G.K. Chesterton's short biography. Then read his *Five Ways* in *Summa Theologiae* I, q.2.

## Critics say

Critics argue his arguments for God depend on an Aristotelian picture of nature that modern science has replaced. His defenders argue they do not.
