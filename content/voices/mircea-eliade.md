## In one idea

Traditional peoples organised their whole world around a sacred centre: a temple, a mountain, a tree that joined heaven and earth. Modern people, he argued, live in a "desacralised" world, yet still long for that centre.

## Why it matters now

His *The Sacred and the Profane* (1957) explains the loss that many feel today, and why rituals, holy places and sacred time matter. Abide's idea of a daily rhythm of prayer is a way of rebuilding sacred time.

## Where to start

*The Sacred and the Profane*.

## Critics say

As a young man in Romania he supported a far-right political movement in the 1930s, a fact scholars continue to weigh when reading his work.
