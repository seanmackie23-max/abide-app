## In one idea

Our hearts are restless until they rest in God. Augustine spent his youth chasing pleasure, career and philosophy, then found that none of them satisfied the hunger underneath. His *Confessions* is the first great autobiography: a prayer addressed to God about his own life.

## Why it matters now

He diagnosed the modern condition 1,600 years early: we love the right things in the wrong order, and wonder why we are unhappy. Anyone who has felt that success left them empty will recognise themselves in him.

## Where to start

*Confessions*, Books I–IX, in a modern translation. Then *City of God* for his vision of history.

## Critics say

His teaching on original sin and predestination has been criticised, including by many Christians, as too harsh.
