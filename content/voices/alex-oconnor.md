## In one idea

Take the strongest arguments on both sides seriously, and follow them where they lead. A former atheist YouTuber, O'Connor now describes himself as agnostic, and interviews believers and sceptics with unusual fairness on his podcast *Within Reason*.

## Why it matters now

Many young seekers first hear thoughtful Christians in his conversations. He models the open, honest searching Abide hopes for.

## Where to start

His podcast *Within Reason*, especially his conversations with Christian thinkers.
