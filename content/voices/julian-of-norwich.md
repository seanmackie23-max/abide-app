## In one idea

God's love is unbreakable: "All shall be well, and all shall be well, and all manner of thing shall be well." During a near-fatal illness Julian received a series of visions of Christ's love, and spent decades reflecting on them in a small cell in Norwich.

## Why it matters now

She wrote in a time of plague, war and fear, and her message is radical hope. Her *Revelations of Divine Love* is the earliest surviving book in English known to be written by a woman.

## Where to start

*Revelations of Divine Love*, the short text first, in a modern English version.
