## In one idea

Conscience is the voice of God in the soul. A leading Oxford Anglican who became a Catholic at great personal cost, Newman explored how real people come to believe: not by one proof, but by many converging reasons.

## Why it matters now

His honest account of changing his mind, *Apologia Pro Vita Sua*, is a model for anyone whose views have shifted. His idea that doctrine develops over time like a living thing is still influential.

## Where to start

*Apologia Pro Vita Sua*, or the hymn "Lead, Kindly Light".
