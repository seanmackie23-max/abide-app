## In one idea

Each night, review your day: what went well, where you fell short, what to do better. Seneca described this practice of nightly self-examination in *On Anger*. Christians later took up the same practice, and Ignatius of Loyola made it the Examen.

## Why it matters now

Stoicism is hugely popular again with people who want a philosophy for living. Seneca is a natural bridge: much of what he sought, Christians believe is fulfilled in Christ.

## Where to start

*Letters from a Stoic*; *On Anger*, Book III, chapter 36, on the evening review.
